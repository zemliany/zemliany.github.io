## The Little Redis Book (UA Translation)

In the programming community, Canadian software developer, author, and speaker Karl Seguin is most recognized for his succinct, easy-to-understand technical manuals. He is the author of several well-reviewed free e-books, one of them - it's "The Little Redis Book" – a beginner-friendly introduction to Redis.

I admit that at one point this book had a certain influence on my development as an engineer in terms of providing an understanding of how to work with different data structures, how to store them, and how to process them effectively. So, perhaps this book will not open your eyes to anything specific, or it may not offer much to experienced engineers. 

But this book will definitely help lay a solid foundation for understanding data and non-relational structures (and we're not just talking about Redis, but rather using it as an example to make it easier to understand) and will give you the opportunity to systematize what you have already learned more deeply, or to pay attention to something and fill in the gaps.
